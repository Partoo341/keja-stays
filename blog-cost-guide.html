Kenya Travel Cost 2026: Complete Budget Guide

Planning a trip to Kenya? Understanding costs is crucial for budgeting. Based on real traveler data, here's everything you need to know about Kenya travel expenses in 2026.

Average Daily Costs in Kenya

  • Budget traveler: $59/day (KES7,628) - hostels, public transport, local eateries
  • Mid-range traveler: $140/day (KES18,096) - Airbnbs, restaurants, activities
  • Luxury traveler: $300+/day (KES38,789) - private villas, gourmet dining, private safaris

Food & Dining Budget

Average daily food costs: $46 (KES5,895)

  • Street food/local cafes: $5-10 per meal
  • Mid-range restaurants: $15-25 per meal
  • Fine dining: $40-80 per meal
  • Grocery shopping (self-catering): $30-50/week

Transportation Costs

Local transportation averages $33/day (KES4,304):

  • Matatu (local bus): $1-5 per trip
  • Uber/taxi: $10-30 per trip
  • Car rental with driver: from KSh8,000/day ($52)
  • Domestic flights: $80-150 one-way
  • Train (Nairobi-Mombasa): $20-40 one-way

Safari Costs

  • Budget group safari (3 days): from KSh40,000 ($260)
  • Mid-range safari (3 days): $600-1,000
  • Luxury private safari: $1,500-3,000+
  • National park entry: from $60/day
