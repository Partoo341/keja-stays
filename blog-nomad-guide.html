Digital Nomad Guide to Nairobi 2026

Why Nairobi for Digital Nomads?

Nairobi has become East Africa's premier digital nomad destination, offering:

  • Fast Internet: Fiber in most neighborhoods (10-200 Mbps)
  • Affordable monthly rentals: KSh 2,000-12,000/night
  • Thriving expat community: Multiple coworking spaces and nomad meetups
  • Perfect time zone: UTC+3 works for US, Europe, and Asia
  • Direct flights: To major global hubs
