Luxury Safari vs Beach: The Ultimate Kenya Combo

Why Choose Both?

Kenya is one of the few destinations where you can experience world-class safari and pristine beaches in a single trip. A combined safari-beach itinerary offers the best of both worlds: thrilling wildlife encounters followed by pure relaxation. The contrast between the savannah and the Indian Ocean creates an unforgettable journey.

12-Day Ultimate Kenya Itinerary

Days 1-3: Nairobi Arrival & Acclimatization

Stay: The翡翠 Residence, Nairobi

Recover from your flight in luxury at our Kilimani property. Visit the David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage and Giraffe Centre while you adjust to the time zone. Enjoy fine dining at renowned Nairobi restaurants just minutes away.

Days 4-8: Masai Mara Safari

Base: The Flamingo Suite, Nakuru (as safari hub)

Safari options:

  • Budget: 3-day group safari from KSh40,000 ($260)
  • Luxury: Private 4WD vehicle and driver from KSh8,000/day ($52)
  • Premium: Hot air balloon safari at dawn + champagne breakfast ($450)

The Masai Mara offers the chance to see the Big Five: lion, leopard, elephant, rhino, and buffalo. During July-October, you might witness the Great Migration.

Days 9-12: Mombasa Beach Relaxation

Stay: Bahari Villa, Mombasa

Unwind at our oceanfront villa with private beach access, infinity pool, and gourmet dining. Take a day trip to Wasini Island for dolphin watching or explore Fort Jesus in Mombasa's old town. Enjoy fresh seafood at local beach restaurants.

Safari vs Beach: Cost Comparison

Experience Budget Luxury
Safari (3 days) $400-600 $1,500-3,000
Beach (4 days) $300-500 $1,200-2,500

Best Time for a Combined Trip

  • January-March: Great weather for both safari and beach, fewer crowds
  • July-October: Peak safari (Great Migration), perfect beach weather
  • December: Holiday season, book 3+ months in advance
