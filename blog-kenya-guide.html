Ultimate Kenya Travel Guide 2026: Where to Stay

Why Kenya in 2026?

Kenya continues to cement its position as Africa's premier travel destination. With new direct flights from major US and European cities, enhanced infrastructure, and world-class hospitality, 2026 is the perfect year to explore the country. Visitors to Kenya spend an average of $140 per day, with accommodation ranging from budget-friendly $33 per night to luxury stays at $300+.

Kenya Travel Costs 2026

  • Budget traveler: $59/day (hostels, public transport, local eateries)
  • Mid-range traveler: $140/day (Airbnbs, restaurants, activities)
  • Luxury traveler: $300+/day (private villas, gourmet dining, private safaris)

When to Visit

Peak season (July-October, December-February): Best wildlife viewing, higher prices

Shoulder seasons (January-March): Great deals, good weather, lower safari prices

Green season (April-June, November): Lowest prices, lush landscapes, fewer tourists
