Ultimate Kenya Travel Guide 2026: Where to Stay

Why Kenya in 2026?

Kenya continues to cement its position as Africa's premier travel destination. With new direct flights from major US and European cities, enhanced infrastructure, and world-class hospitality, 2026 is the perfect year to explore the country. Visitors to Kenya spend an average of $140 per day, with accommodation ranging from budget-friendly $33 per night to luxury stays at $300+.

Safari vs Beach: Can You Have Both?

Absolutely! A 12-day itinerary combining safari and beach is the ultimate Kenya experience. Start with 5-6 days in the Masai Mara for wildlife viewing, then head to the coast for relaxation. Our properties in Nakuru serve as perfect safari bases, while Bahari Villa in Mombasa offers beachfront luxury.

When to Visit

  • Peak season (July-October): Best wildlife viewing, higher prices
  • Shoulder seasons (January-March): Great deals, good weather
  • Green season (April-June): Lowest prices, lush landscapes
